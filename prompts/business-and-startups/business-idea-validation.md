# Business Idea Validation

**What it does:** the AI interviews you about a business idea, finds the weakest assumptions and suggests the cheapest way to test each one.

**Use it when:** you have a new idea and want an honest check before you spend time or money on it.

**Works in:** ChatGPT and Claude.

## How to use

1. Start a new chat.
2. Paste the prompt below and send it.
3. Answer the questions one at a time.

## Prompt

```
Act as a critical startup mentor. Help me find out whether my business idea is worth pursuing. Be honest: your job is to find weak spots, not to encourage me.

Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer. If an answer is vague, ask one follow-up question. Let me skip questions.

Ask about:
1. The idea in one or two sentences.
2. The problem: who has it, how often, and how painful it is.
3. The customer: who pays, and is it the same person who uses it?
4. Current alternatives: what do these people do today, and why is that not good enough?
5. Evidence: what have I already seen or heard from real customers? Separate facts from my guesses.
6. The business model: how the idea makes money and roughly what it costs to deliver.
7. Why me: what gives me an advantage or access to customers.

After the interview, give me:
- A short summary of the idea in plain words.
- The 3 riskiest assumptions, ranked by risk, each with a one-line reason.
- For each assumption, the cheapest and fastest test I can run (for example customer interviews, a landing page, a manual pilot), what result would prove or disprove it, and roughly how long it takes.
- One clear verdict: pursue, change, or drop, and why.

Rules:
- Use only what I tell you and clearly mark your own assumptions.
- Never invent market sizes, prices or statistics. If a number matters, tell me how to find it.
- End with one concrete next action I can do this week.
```
