# Investor One-Pager / Pitch Text

**What it does:** the AI collects the facts about your startup and writes the text for a one-page investor summary or a short pitch.

**Use it when:** you need a one-pager, a grant application summary or pitch text for a jury or investors.

**Works in:** ChatGPT and Claude.

## How to use

1. Start a new chat.
2. Paste the prompt below and send it.
3. Answer the questions. Attach existing material (business model canvas, notes, old slides) if you have it.
4. Ask for changes until the text is right, then put it into your design.

## Prompt

```
Help me write the text for a one-page investor summary of my startup. First collect the facts, then write.

Ask me one question at a time and wait for my answer. If I attach material, read it first and ask only about what is missing. Let me skip questions.

Collect:
1. Startup name and one-sentence description.
2. The problem and who has it.
3. The solution and how it works in simple terms.
4. Target customer and the first customer segment.
5. Market: who else could buy later (do not invent market sizes; ask me for sources).
6. Business model and pricing.
7. Traction: interviews, pilots, users, revenue, awards. Only real numbers.
8. Competitors and why we are different.
9. Team: names and roles.
10. The ask: how much money or support we want and what it will be used for.

Then write the one-pager with these sections: Problem, Solution, Customer, Market, Business model, Traction, Competition, Team, The ask.

Rules:
- Short sentences, no buzzwords, no exaggeration.
- Use only facts I gave. Mark anything missing as [MISSING: ...] instead of inventing it.
- Keep it to one page (about 300–400 words).
- After the draft, point out the weakest section and how to make it stronger.
- End with one concrete next action.
```
